Long-Video Analysis & Summarisation

Load a video of any length, up to a 30-minute lecture or longer, and get its key frames, scene boundaries, a motion map, tracked people and objects, and a short summary video. Frames are sampled and processed in your browser. The video is never uploaded.

Scenes are ranked by length, motion, visual distinctiveness and detected objects. The highest-ranked scenes each contribute a clip centred on their most active moment, and the clips play in their original order.

Method

Frame sampling

The video is sampled by seeking to evenly spaced timestamps. Auto mode picks 4 fps for clips under 3 minutes, 2 fps under 10 minutes, 1 fps under 40 minutes and 0.5 fps beyond that, with at most 3,600 samples. A 30-minute video therefore becomes about 1,800 frames. Each frame is scaled to 160 px wide, giving a 16×4×4 HSV histogram, a grayscale image and a Laplacian sharpness value.

Scene boundaries and key frames
  • Dissimilarity D = 0.75 · Bhattacharyya distance of HSV histograms + 0.25 · 2 · mean absolute pixel difference.
  • Cuts: local maxima above an adaptive threshold max(0.28, μ + kσ), with μ and σ taken from ±8 neighbouring samples. The sensitivity slider sets k from 4 down to 2.
  • Gradual transitions (fades, dissolves): twin-comparison. A run of moderately high D is accepted when its first and last frames differ by more than the cut threshold.
  • Key frame = scene medoid (closest to the scene's mean histogram) with a sharpness bonus. Long scenes get extra key frames when content drifts by more than 0.3.
Motion map

Absolute differences between consecutive samples (excluding pairs that span a cut) are accumulated per pixel into a motion energy image and normalised. Mean difference per sample gives the motion-intensity curve.

Tracking
  • COCO-SSD (TensorFlow.js, MobileNet v2 lite) detects people or all 80 COCO classes in every sampled frame. The model downloads once (~5 MB) and runs on your GPU through WebGL.
  • Moving blobs: the background of each scene is the per-pixel temporal median. Foreground mask = |frame − background| > 28, then two dilations and 8-connected components. Frames where more than 40% of the image moves are treated as camera motion and skipped.
  • A greedy tracker matches detections across samples using constant-velocity prediction, centre distance and IoU, and resets at every scene cut.
Summary

Importance = 0.3 · log-duration + 0.3 · motion + 0.25 · distinctiveness (histogram distance from the whole video) + 0.15 · detections, each normalised to 0–1. Clips are taken from scenes in order of importance until the target length is reached. The summary can be played in place or recorded to WebM with MediaRecorder.
